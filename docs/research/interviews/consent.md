# Consent approach

Status: **draft, needs approval** before the first session. Before approving, check it against local rules for research with children, for example the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and UK GDPR, or COPPA in the US.

## Who consents

| Segment       | Consent from              | Assent from                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Children 8–12 | Parent or carer (written) | Child, spoken, using the script below |
| Teens 13–17   | Parent or carer (written) | Teen (written)                        |
| Adults        | Participant (written)     | —                                     |
| Families      | Each adult (written)      | Each child, spoken                    |

## Parent or adult consent form

> We are designing an app that helps people learn the science behind everyday objects by taking a photo. We'd like to talk with you (or your child) for about 30–45 minutes about curiosity and learning, and to watch you try an early prototype.
>
> - Taking part is voluntary. You or your child can stop at any time, skip any question, and withdraw afterwards without giving a reason.
> - We won't take photos or video of anyone's face. With your permission we'll make an audio recording so our notes are accurate. We delete it once the notes are checked, within 30 days.
> - The prototype doesn't store any photos you take. It records only which buttons are pressed and answers to short quiz questions, under a code instead of a name.
> - Notes use a code, never your name. Anonymised findings may be shared within the project team and in internal reports.
> - Questions or withdrawal: contact \_\_\_ (email).
>
> ☐ I agree to take part / for my child to take part
> ☐ I agree to an audio recording (optional)
> Name, signature, date

## Child assent script (read aloud)

> "We're making an app that explains how things work. We want to know what you think, and there are no wrong answers. We're testing the app, not you. If you want to stop or skip something, just say so and that's totally fine. Is it OK if we chat for a bit?"

Continue only after a clear yes. Stop if the child seems uncomfortable, even if the parent wants to carry on.

## Data retention

| Item                         | Where kept                      | Deleted                        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Consent forms                | Access-controlled drive         | 12 months after the study ends |
| Code-to-name key             | Separate access-controlled file | When synthesis is approved     |
| Audio                        | Local encrypted device          | Within 30 days                 |
| Raw notes, prototype exports | Access-controlled drive         | 12 months after the study ends |
| Anonymised synthesis         | This repo                       | Kept                           |

## Incentive

A small thank-you (for example a gift voucher). Give it whether or not the session is completed.
