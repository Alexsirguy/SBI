# Screener

Send as a short form. For under-18s, a parent or carer fills it in.

1. Who would take part?
   - My child, aged 8–12 (I'll be there too)
   - My teenager, aged 13–17
   - Me (adult)
   - Me and my child together
2. Age of the learner taking part: \_\_\_
3. In the last month, how often has the learner asked "how does that work?" about something around them?
   - Never · Once or twice · Weekly · Most days
4. Where do they usually look for an answer? (tick all)
   - Ask a person · Search engine · YouTube or TikTok · An AI chatbot · Books · Don't look it up
5. Does the learner use a phone or tablet with a camera? Yes / No
6. Any accessibility needs we should plan for (for example screen reader, larger text, captions)? \_\_\_
7. Best times and contact email (parent's email for under-18s).

## Selection

- Spread participants across curiosity levels (Q3) and how they look things up (Q4); don't only recruit science fans.
- In each segment, include at least one learner who doesn't usually look answers up.
- Exclude household members of the team and anyone who has seen the prototype.
