# Teacher interviews and classroom privacy (E13-1)

Goal: find out whether teachers would use the app with a class, what they need to control, and what a school must be told before it agrees. Epic #111 asked for this before any classroom feature was built. **The owner chose to build first (2026-11-08)**, so these sessions now check the shipped design instead of gating it.

Take notes with the learner study's [notes template](../interviews/notes-template.md), using teacher codes (`P01` primary, `J01` junior secondary, `S01` senior secondary, `L01` school leader or IT lead).

## Plan

| Segment                       | Who                                              | Target sessions | Country        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------- | -------------- |
| Primary (ages 8–11)           | Class teachers who teach science                 | 3–4             | US and Nigeria |
| Junior secondary (JSS / 6–8)  | Basic Science and Technology or science teachers | 3–4             | US and Nigeria |
| Senior secondary (SSS / 9–12) | Maths, physics, chemistry or biology teachers    | 4–5             | US and Nigeria |
| School leaders and IT         | Heads, deputies or whoever approves new apps     | 2–3             | US and Nigeria |

Aim for at least a third of sessions in each country, so the curriculum switch (US NGSS/Common Core and Nigeria NERDC) is tested by people who teach it.

## Data rules

The learner study's [data rules](../interviews/README.md#data-rules) apply. In addition, never record the names of pupils, even when a teacher mentions them; write "a pupil".
