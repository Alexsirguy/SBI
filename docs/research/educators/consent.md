# Consent approach

Status: **draft, needs approval** before the first session. Participants are adult teachers, so no child consent is needed. **No pupils take part and no classroom is observed.**

## Teacher consent form

> We are designing an app that explains the science behind everyday objects, and we are adding a way for teachers to share lessons with a class. We'd like to talk with you for about 45 minutes about how you teach and to watch you try an early version.
>
> - Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, skip any question, and withdraw afterwards without giving a reason.
> - With your permission we'll make an audio recording so our notes are accurate. We delete it once the notes are checked, within 30 days.
> - Notes use a code, never your name or your school's name. Please don't name pupils; if you do, we won't write it down.
> - Anonymised findings may be shared within the project team and in internal reports.
> - Questions or withdrawal: contact \_\_\_ (email).
>
> ☐ I agree to take part
> ☐ I agree to an audio recording (optional)
> Name, signature, date

## School permission

If a teacher wants to try the app with a real class after the interview, that is a separate step. The school must agree in writing first, using the one-page summary in [privacy-legal-design.md](privacy-legal-design.md#one-page-summary-for-schools). The interview itself needs only the teacher's consent.
