# Interview guide

About 45 minutes. Follow the teacher's own stories more than the order of the script.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)

- Introductions, consent check, reminder that they can stop any time.
- "Tell me about the classes you teach this term."

## 2. A real lesson (10 min)

- "Walk me through the last time a pupil asked how something worked and you didn't have time to answer properly."
- "When you bring in something outside the scheme of work, how do you justify it? Who checks?"
- "How do you link a lesson to the curriculum today?" Listen for NGSS codes, NERDC themes, WAEC topics or nothing at all.

## 3. Devices and connection (5 min)

- "What happens when the internet drops in the middle of a lesson?"
- "If pupils used this, would it be on their phones, shared devices, or just your screen?"

## 4. Control and trust (10 min)

- "Before pupils see a lesson, what would you need to check?"
- "Which topics would you want to switch off for a class?" Note whether they mention medicine, chemicals, weapons, self-harm or biology hazards.
- "Would you want pupils to ask the tutor questions on their own?"
- "What would your head or the parents need to know first?"
- "What would you want to see about how a class is doing? What would feel like too much?" Listen for any wish to see individual pupils; we deliberately don't offer it.

## 5. Prototype tasks (12 min)

Think-aloud throughout; don't help unless they're stuck for more than 30 seconds.

1. **Make a class:** create a class, set the curriculum (US or Nigeria) and level, and copy the link.
2. **Add lessons:** add two saved lessons. Do the curriculum links match what they would teach?
3. **Controls:** lock the age band, switch off a topic, turn off the tutor. Is anything missing?
4. **Pupil view:** open the class link in a private window. "Is this what you expected pupils to see?"
5. **Offline:** save a lesson for offline use, switch the connection off and open it.
6. **Class summary:** look at the summary page. "Would this help you? What would you do with it?"

## 6. Wrap-up (3 min)

- "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
- "Would you use this with a class next term? What would stop you?"
- Thank them and give the incentive.
