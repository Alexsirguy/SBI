# Screener

Send as a short form.

1. Which do you teach? (tick all)
   - Primary science · Basic Science and Technology (JSS) · Middle-school science · SSS / high-school Mathematics · Physics · Chemistry · Biology · I lead or approve apps for a school
2. Country and type of school: Nigeria / US / other; public, private, mission or faith, other.
3. Class size, roughly: under 20 · 20–40 · 41–60 · over 60.
4. Which curriculum do you follow? NGSS · Common Core · NERDC · WAEC/NECO syllabus · state standards · other.
5. How do your pupils get to a device in lessons?
   - Their own phones · Shared tablets or laptops · A computer lab · Only my device, shown on a screen or projector · None
6. How reliable is the internet where you teach? Always · Usually · Sometimes · Rarely.
7. Have you used an app or website with a class in the last term? Which?
8. Best times and contact email.

## Selection

- Include teachers with poor or no connectivity (Q6) and teachers whose pupils share devices or have none (Q5). These test offline packs and teacher-led use.
- Spread across class sizes; large Nigerian classes are a different problem from a US class of 25.
- Exclude anyone who has seen the prototype.
