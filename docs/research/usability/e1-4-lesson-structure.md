# Usability findings: 8-module lesson structure (E1-4)

Status: **not started.** Run with the prototype in facilitator mode. Each lesson gives the short answer first, then builds it up from first principles, one step at a time. Half of the participants get variant **A** (guess before each step's answer is shown), the other half **B** (the same steps with the answers shown, no guessing). Assign A to odd participant codes and B to even.

## What we're testing

1. **Quick answer first:** is the short answer enough for some learners, and do others choose to go deeper?
2. **First-principles steps:** after the short answer, do learners tap **Show me why**? How far through the steps do they go (the `step` events), and where do they stop?
3. **Depth control:** do learners find "Simply / Normally / In depth", and which do they choose (the `depth` events)?
4. **Guessing:** does A (guess at each step) or B (read only) lead to more steps opened, better post-check answers and better explain-backs? Which guesses do learners get wrong (the `guess` events)?
5. **Evidence:** do learners open **How do we know?** (the `evidence` events)?
6. **Concept check:** the same question before and after (`precheck` and `postcheck`), plus a spoken explain-back noted by the facilitator.

## Topics

| Topic   | Check question                                            | Common misconception tested            |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Bicycle | On a steep hill, which gear makes pedalling easiest?      | A bigger front ring makes hills easier |
| Kettle  | What are the big bubbles in boiling water mostly made of? | The bubbles are air                    |
| Leaf    | Where does most of the wood in a tree come from?          | Plants get their mass from soil        |
