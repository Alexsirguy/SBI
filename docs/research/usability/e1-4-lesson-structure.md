# Usability findings: 8-module lesson structure (E1-4)

Status: **not started.** Run with the prototype in facilitator mode. Half of the participants get module order **A** (spec order: big idea before parts), the other half **B** (parts and a hands-on idea before the big idea). Assign A to odd participant codes and B to even.

## What we're testing

1. **Quick answer first:** is the short answer enough for some learners, and do others choose to go deeper?
2. **Progressive depth:** how far do learners go (the `module` events), and where do they stop?
3. **Depth control:** do learners find "Simply / Normally / In depth", and which do they choose (the `depth` events)?
4. **Module order:** does A or B lead to more modules opened and better post-check answers?
5. **Concept check:** the same question before and after (`precheck` and `postcheck`), plus a spoken explain-back noted by the facilitator.

## Topics

| Topic   | Check question                                            | Common misconception tested            |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Bicycle | On a steep hill, which gear makes pedalling easiest?      | A bigger front ring makes hills easier |
| Kettle  | What are the big bubbles in boiling water mostly made of? | The bubbles are air                    |
| Leaf    | Where does most of the wood in a tree come from?          | Plants get their mass from soil        |
