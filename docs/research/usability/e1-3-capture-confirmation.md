# Usability findings: photo capture and confirmation (E1-3)

Status: **not started.** Needs at least 5 learners. Sessions follow the prototype tasks in [the interview guide](../interviews/guide.md).

## What we're testing

1. **Confident path (bicycle):** do learners confirm quickly, and do they notice the alternatives?
2. **Ambiguous path (shiny pot):** do they understand why we're asking, and pick the right one?
3. **Unsupported path (tablets):** is the redirect clear and does it feel kind rather than broken?
4. **Correction:** can they use "Something else?" without help?

## Measures, from the facilitator export

| Measure                                                        | Target                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Time from `recognition` to `confirm` on the confident path     | under 5 s             |
| Ambiguous path: picked the intended candidate                  | at least 4 of 5       |
| Unsupported path: tapped "Try another photo" without prompting | at least 4 of 5       |
| Facilitator help needed (from notes)                           | at most 1 per session |
