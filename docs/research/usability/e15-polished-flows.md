# Usability round: polished capture and lesson (E15-7, #260)

Status: **not started.** It needs at least 5 learners, run against the polished app (epic #253) rather than the Phase 0 prototype. Sessions follow [the interview guide](../interviews/guide.md) for consent, and are recorded only with permission.

The round also covers the open E1-3 and E1-4 questions, because the polished flows now do what those prototypes tested. Each session takes about 25 minutes, on the participant's own phone where possible.

## Tasks

1. **Start from home.** "Find out the science behind something on your desk." Watch which path they choose and whether they read the photo note.
2. **Capture.** Take a photo of an everyday object, crop it if needed, and upload it.
3. **Confirm.** Pick the right object, or correct it with their own words.
4. **Read.** Read the quick answer, then go one level deeper. Ask them to find where a fact came from.
5. **Change depth.** Switch between Quick and Deeper. Ask what changed.
6. **Check your thinking.** Answer the question, then compare with the explanation.
7. **Give feedback.** Rate the lesson, and report anything that looked wrong.

## Measures

| Measure                                                              | Target                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Home to "Start lesson" without help                                  | at least 4 of 5       |
| Quick answer explained back correctly in their own words             | at least 4 of 5       |
| Found a fact's source from its marker                                | at least 4 of 5       |
| Understood the "general knowledge" or "not reviewed" note when asked | at least 4 of 5       |
| Depth change noticed and described                                   | at least 3 of 5       |
| Facilitator help needed                                              | at most 1 per session |
| Explain-back quality, before and after the lesson (0–2, as E1-4)     | rises for most        |
